# Corrections

## 2026-10-28 — errata to ferromagnetic-full-threshold-glauber 2.0.0-candidate (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23359097)

The archived PDF, the manuscript source, the Zenodo deposit and the tag `v2.0.0-candidate` are unchanged. These errata were confirmed by the Evidence Press archive audit and are also shown at <https://evidencepress.org/releases/ferromagnetic-full-threshold-glauber/>.

1. Corrected an overbroad sentence in the abstract. The archived text says “individual edge and vertex parameters need not be bounded away from zero or infinity”. Hypothesis (2) requires 0 < λ_v < λ, 0 < β_e ≤ β and 1 < β_eγ_e ≤ βγ, so the vertex fugacities λ_v and the edge parameters β_e are bounded above; only the individual γ_e may be arbitrarily large. As §8 states, the sentence should say that λ_v and β_e may be arbitrarily small and γ_e arbitrarily large.

   Only the abstract’s description of the parameter class is affected; Theorem 1 and its hypotheses are unchanged. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.
