# Citation audit

The three versioned arXiv sources in [SOURCES](SOURCES.md) were checked at the statement/definition level relevant to their use on 14 October 2026.

- FGY's influence theorem applies to the heterogeneous ferromagnetic class, not only its RBM special case. Incoming orientation agrees with the manuscript. The prior near-linear result has a smaller field range.
- Miao–Yin's entropy theorem requires occupied-marginal lower bounds under positive pinning. Its gap proposition does not. The paper makes this distinction and applies entropy only after trimming.
- CFYZ's marginal stability compares conditional odds before/after additional pins. The star calculation therefore addresses the correct quantity and direction. Its v1 identifier is fixed in the bibliography.

Source existence alone would not establish applicability. The audit does not certify global priority, peer-review status of the sources, or every step of their proofs. Very recent preprints admit possible concurrent developments. No citation has been counted as independent reproduction.
